# EP2: replacement scenes and additional shots

These pages are written in the same format as `EPISODE_2_INTERACTIVE_SCRIPT`. Each one names the print it replaces, what changed and why, and the **cliffhanger as it now appears on screen**. The analysis is in [`EP2_STORY_ANALYSIS.md`](EP2_STORY_ANALYSIS.md). The render-ready files are in [`pika/shots/`](pika/shots/).

**Order in the episode:** C1 hook → 00 → title → card → 01 (+P1) → 02 → 03 → **04 v5** → 05 → 06 (+P2) → 07 → **08 v2** → 09 → 10 → 11 → 12 → 13 → **14 v3** (+C3, clue replay) → 15 (+C2) → **16 v4** → **17 v4** → P3 → 18

## C1 — COLD OPEN: "THE SUGAR" *(new · insert reel 2, 0:00–0:10)*

**INT. COLD CARRIAGE — MORNING · 1 shot · 10 s**

Breakfast service in the warm first-class carriage. The camera dollies down the aisle at seated-eye height, past passengers, steam and teapots, toward the right-hand window table. A man sits there **frozen solid**, with rime radiating from his sternum and his teapot still steaming. Nobody looks at him. A passenger at the next table leans across without looking, takes the sugar bowl from his table and says, pleasantly:

> PASSENGER: Thank you.
>
> ANNOUNCEMENT (V.O.): The Hessel crossing is warm and will remain warm. Passengers are thanked for their continued regard.

Smash to black.

**Why:** it puts the episode's impossible image and its question in the first second, and gets a laugh in the first ten. It also states the theme as an image before anyone says it: everyone in the room was drawing warmth from him and nobody noticed. The announcement now lies twice, here and in Sc01.

> **Hang the cliff:** *A dead man at breakfast, and somebody just borrowed his sugar.*

## 00 — PRE-INTRO: SAFETY INFORMATION v3 *(replaces v2 · `ep2_scene00_preintro_v3`)*

v2 is kept word for word, with one structural change:

| | v2 (print) | v3 |
|---|---|---|
| Shot 3 (0:17–0:25) | "Your ticket…" → "I—" → hold → "The railway regrets…" all in one shot | "Your ticket…" → **"I—"** → two seconds of silence → **CUT** |
| Shot 4 (0:25–0:30) | card back in the pocket, passenger sits | **opens on** "The railway regrets nothing it has done correctly." → card back, passenger sits |

**Why:** in v2 the stop collapsed into *"I, the Railway, regrets…"*. The notes are clear (§18/§66): a dramatic pause is only reliable on a shot boundary. The aborted first person is the only reason this scene exists.

> **Hang the cliff:** *It started to say "I" and stopped. The railway regrets nothing it has done correctly.*

**Card after the main title:** `06:02 — THAT MORNING`, typeset in Resolve and held 2 s. In Sc09 the docket reads `BOARDED 06:02`, so viewers who catch it are rewarded.

## P1 — SC01 TAG: "HIS HAND" *(new · insert reel 1, 0:00–0:10)*

**EXT. HESSEL PIER — DAWN · CU 100mm, locked · 10 s · no dialogue**

Kell's hand comes out of the one dark slot. The slots on either side glow. **A fine fresh rime is on his knuckles** and it grows a little as we watch. He turns the hand over once, closes it, and the fist goes into his coat pocket.

**Edit:** cut it into the Sc01 v2 print at the moment he looks at his hand, then go back to his look up the pier.

**Why:** the clue is now visible without anyone acknowledging it. The viewer asks *"what was that?"*, which is the question the episode wants. It also plants the frost-outward rule two scenes early. It is used again in the Sc14 clue replay.

> **Hang the cliff:** *Every hand on that pier came out of the wall warm. His came out with frost on it, and he got on the train.*

## 04 — THE FROST GROWS OUTWARD v5 *(replaces v4 · `ep2_scene04_v5` + plate `ep2_kell_alive_still`)*

v4's structure (aperture plane, pale coat, Tomas large) is kept, with one fix: **inside the oval, Kell is alive.**

* **New plate** `ep2_kell_alive_lock`: derived image-to-image from the Sc03 frame, so face, coat, seat and carriage match. Only his condition changes: eyes open, living colour, a pen moving, cold reconstruction light, no frost.
* The Sc03 frame now owns **face, coat, seat and carriage, not his condition**. That is the precedence error that made v4 show a corpse.
* Shot 2 names the living state at the start and at the end: *"AT THE END OF THE SHOT HE IS STILL ALIVE AND STILL WRITING, his hands rimed white."*

| Shot | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 1 MS 40mm push | 0:00–0:06 | Beatrice raises the oval; inside it the image is cold, outside it warm. |
| 2 insert through the oval, locked, uncut | 0:06–0:17 | **Kell alive, writing.** Frost grows out of his sternum across his coat, the cloth and his writing hands. He never stops writing. Amina (O.S.): *"That's the wrong direction." / "Cold enters a body. It doesn't leave one…"* |
| 3 CU Tomas, large | 0:17–0:23 | He arrives from the pier with **no breath**. Nobody notices. |
| 4 two-shot by frame side | 0:23–0:30 | *"He was not chilled. He was withdrawn from." / "By what?" / "By something with an account."* |

**Act I ends here.** The solution is on screen: a living man, frost growing outward from him, and a word, *account*.

> **Hang the cliff:** *A man sat in a warm room doing paperwork while something took the heat out of him, and he didn't stop writing.*

## P2 — SC06 BUTTON: "AND NOBODY LOOKED UP" *(new · insert reel 1, 0:10–0:20)*

**EXT. PIER HEAD, UNDER THE BOARD · CU 85mm, locked · 10 s**

Beatrice looks up at the board, cold light, breath visible:

> BEATRICE: And nobody looked up.

Two seconds of silence.

**Edit:** this replaces the garbled end of Sc06 Shot 3 (*"And nobody else."*). Follow it with 2 s of the existing Sc06 Shot 1 wide: forty people sitting with their backs to the board.

**Why:** the scene's verdict line, and its button, were lost in the render. A one-line shot with protected silence is the form the notes say always survives (§99).

> **Hang the cliff:** *The other end of the line has been gone for six days, the board never said so, and forty people are sitting underneath it waiting to cross.*

> **Hang the cliff:** *Pikk said no, she didn't ask anyone else, and she is going to pay it herself.*

> **Hang the cliff:** *He said the true thing out loud to keep her out of the wall, and now it is a fact in the world, and he cannot unsay it.*

## P3 — 17→18 BRIDGE: "YSRA BOARDS LAST" *(new · insert reel 1, 0:20–0:30)*

**EXT. PIER, CARRIAGE DOOR · MS 50mm, very slow push · 10 s · no dialogue**

Everyone else has boarded. Ysra stands at the step, **head bare, the coiled veil carried in her gloved hand like a hat she has taken off**, the ring-dent visible in her braids. She looks back down the pier toward the Reciprocity House for a long moment, face unmanaged. Then she steps up into the warm doorway, and **her face changes from cold to warm light as she crosses**. She does not look back.

**Why:** her arc ended off screen at 480p. Now it ends on a readable image, using the Sc02 colour-temperature rule one last time.

> **Hang the cliff:** *She is going home without the face she was issued, and everyone on that train is about to see it.*

## What carries over unchanged

Sc02, 05, 07, 09, 10, 11, 12 (trim the doubled "Stop"), 13 and 18 stay as printed. The free edit changes (Sc03 still, Sc11 flash, act cards, end tease) are listed in §7 of the analysis.

### Continuity notes for the editor
* **Beatrice's frost** appears from C2 (Sc15) onward: knuckles, then throat. In Sc17 her fingertips whiten only at the nail beds before Tomas lifts her hand out, so Sc18 v2 (her hands in her lap) still matches.
* **Pikk's right hand** stays in his pocket or white from Sc08 onward. Sc09's print breaks this; it is minor, so leave it.
* **Tomas's costume** is correct in every new file (verified plate, large framing). The old wing collar remains in the Sc05/06/09 prints. Re-rolling those is optional and costs about $10.
